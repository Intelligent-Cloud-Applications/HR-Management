Complete Functional & Technical Specifications Document. TekkzyWork consolidates workforce operations, shift tracking, Indian statutory payroll (TDS, PF, PT), talent acquisition ATS, and performance OKRs into a single high-density platform.

1. Executive Overview & Key Differentiators

TekkzyWork is designed to bridge the gap between heavy enterprise legacy ERP systems and fragile spreadsheets. Built with a data-forward design philosophy, it allows HR Operations, Finance teams, and Department Leads to execute high-volume workforce tasks with speed and zero layout bloat.

⚡ High-Density UI UX
Compact table density, hairline borders, and inline modal drawers optimized for viewing 100+ records simultaneously without excessive scrolling.
🇮🇳 Indian Statutory Engine Payroll
Built-in calculation rules for Provident Fund (EPFO 12%), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), and TDS Income Tax Slabs.
🎯 Integrated ATS & OKRs Talent
End-to-end recruitment Kanban pipeline combined with strategic objective scorecards for quarterly employee evaluations.

5. Detailed Functional Modules & Specifications (13 Modules)

Module 1: Public Marketing & Pricing Landing Page (/) Public Route

Primary Objective: High-converting SaaS landing page for client acquisition and product showcase.

  • Hero Section: Engaging headline, CTAs for demo requests, and interactive live product preview mockup.
  • Core Feature Matrix: 6 grid tiles detailing Workforce Management, Statutory Payroll, Geofencing, Talent ATS, OKRs, and Form 16 Compliance.
  • Interactive Pricing Calculator: Real-time price calculation slider ($/₹ per employee/month) with tier options (Growth, Enterprise).
  • Corporate Footer: Legal links, Tekkzy Cloud copyright, and status indicators.
Module 2: SaaS Authentication Portal (/login) Auth Route

Primary Objective: Secure authentication gateway with quick-switch demo profiles.

  • Demo Persona Quick-Fill: 1-click buttons to instantly populate credentials for HR Admin (admin@tekkzy.com), HR Lead, and Staff Employee.
  • Form Validation: Real-time email validation and password visibility toggle.
  • Session Handling: Redirects authenticated users directly to /dashboard.
Module 3: Executive Analytics Dashboard (/dashboard) Core Module

Primary Objective: High-level operational overview for executive management.

  • Real-Time KPI Cards: Total Headcount, Active Today, Monthly Payroll Outflow, Pending Leave Approvals.
  • Headcount Growth Area Chart: Monthly growth trend visualization using Recharts.
  • Department Breakdown Pie/Bar: Headcount distribution across Engineering, Product, HR, Finance, and Sales.
  • Weekly Attendance Trends: On-time vs Late arrivals visual analysis.
Module 4: High-Density Employee Directory (/dashboard/employees) Core Module

Primary Objective: Master directory for managing complete employee lifecycles.

  • Instant Search & Multi-Filter: Filter by name, email, department, status (Active, On Leave, Offboarding), and employment type.
  • Slide-Out Profile Drawer: View detailed personal info, emergency contacts, compensation tier, and manager reporting line.
  • Onboarding Modal: Form trigger for adding new hires with input validations.
  • Batch CSV Export: Export active directory data to downloadable CSV format.
Module 5: Geofenced Attendance & Live Ticker (/dashboard/attendance) Core Module

Primary Objective: Real-time shift monitoring and geo-verification time tracking.

  • Active Shift Ticker: Live stopwatch showing hours and minutes logged during current shift.
  • Clock In / Clock Out Action: Interactive status toggle with GPS geofence verification badge.
  • Daily Logs Table: View arrival timestamp, departure timestamp, total hours, and overtime calculations.
Module 6: Leave Quota & Approval Engine (/dashboard/leave) Core Module

Primary Objective: Managing leave allocations, balances, and manager workflows.

  • Quota Balance Cards: Visual progress bars for Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), Earned Leave (EL), and Parental Leave.
  • Leave Application Modal: Select leave category, date range, half-day/full-day toggle, and reason document upload.
  • Manager Approval Action Strip: 1-click Approve / Reject actions with real-time balance recalculation.
Module 7: Statutory Payroll & Payslip Engine (/dashboard/payroll) Statutory Compliance

Primary Objective: Monthly salary calculations, tax compliance, and automated payslip generation.

  • Batch Disbursement Engine: 1-click process to trigger August 2026 payroll batch for all active employees.
  • Deductions Breakdown: Automatic calculation of Basic, HRA, Special Allowances, PF (12%), ESI, PT, and Income Tax (TDS).
  • Printable Payslips Modal: Downloadable, printable formal salary slip template compliant with Indian tax regulations.
Module 8: Recruitment ATS & Candidate Kanban (/dashboard/recruitment) Talent Acquisition

Primary Objective: Applicant tracking pipeline from requisition to hiring.

  • 5-Stage Kanban Pipeline: Drag-and-drop / stage progression across Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, and Hired.
  • Candidate Profiles: Resume view, interviewer notes, technical assessment scores, and expected CTC.
  • Requisitions Manager: Open job postings count, target hiring dates, and assigned recruiter.
Module 9: Performance Management & OKRs (/dashboard/performance) Core Module

Primary Objective: Alignment of individual performance with corporate key results.

  • Company Strategic OKRs: Progress indicators for company-wide objectives (e.g., "Scale Infrastructure to 99.99% Uptime").
  • 360-Degree Review Scorecards: Self-evaluations, peer feedback ratings, and manager score metrics.
  • Growth Track Indicators: Promotion readiness and skill matrix badges.
Module 10: Department Directory & Budgeting (/dashboard/departments) Core Module

Primary Objective: Organizational structure mapping and headcount budget management.

  • Department Cards: Headcount count, Department Head assignment, total annual budget allocation.
  • Cost Center Tracking: Utilization percentages against approved fiscal budgets.
Module 11: Enterprise Reports & Form 16 Export (/dashboard/reports) Compliance & Analytics

Primary Objective: Executive analytics export and statutory tax reporting.

  • Attrition Benchmarking: Quarter-over-quarter turnover analysis.
  • Statutory Exports: EPFO ECR file export trigger, Form 16 generation, and Monthly PT statements.
Module 12: Development Log & Strategic Roadmap (/dashboard/dev-log) Internal Roadmap

Primary Objective: Transparent tracking of system updates and feature rollouts.

  • Changelog Timeline: Shipped milestones tagged with version numbers and date stamps.
  • Upcoming Checklist: Planned modules (e.g., Slack Integration, Biometric Sync) with status tags.
Module 13: System Settings & Legal Entity Info (/dashboard/settings) Admin Configuration

Primary Objective: Platform configuration and security settings.

  • Admin Profile: Personal details, avatar upload, and email preferences.
  • Company Legal Information: CIN, GSTIN, PAN, and registered corporate address details.
  • Security & 2FA: Password changes, active sessions list, and Two-Factor Authentication toggle.

7. Statutory Payroll Calculation Rules (Indian Labor Law)

TekkzyWork enforces standard Indian statutory payroll formulas within the calculation engine:

Component Statutory Formula / Rule Standard Capping Limit
Provident Fund (EPFO) 12% of (Basic Salary) for Employee contribution Capped at ₹1,800/month (if Basic > ₹15,000) unless voluntary option checked
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Minimum of: (a) Actual HRA, (b) 50% Basic (Metro), (c) Rent paid - 10% Basic Subject to Rent Receipts submission
Professional Tax (PT) Slab-based state tax (e.g., Karnataka/Maharashtra) Max ₹200/month (₹300 in Feb)
Income Tax Withholding (TDS) Calculated under New Tax Regime slabs (Section 115BAC) Standard Deduction of ₹75,000 applied annually

9. Troubleshooting & FAQ

How do I reset or switch demo roles during testing?
Navigate to /login or click the Profile Avatar in the top navbar and select "Switch Role". Select between HR Admin, HR Lead, and Employee to test privilege guards.
Why are payslip print dialogs not showing background styles?
In your browser's Print Dialog, ensure the checkbox "Background Graphics" is enabled under More Settings.
How is dark mode persisted across sessions?
The theme selection (`light` | `dark` | `system`) is automatically saved to the browser's localStorage.setItem('tekkzy_theme', ...).
